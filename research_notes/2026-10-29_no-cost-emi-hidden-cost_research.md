## Research notes
- Lender interest is offset by an upfront seller or brand discount, so visible interest reads zero.
- GST on interest and processing fees on conversion are widely reported.
- Reward points are commonly excluded on EMI purchases.
- RBI is reported to have said in 2013 that zero-percent schemes camouflage interest; wording and date unconfirmed.
