## Research notes
- Common explanation: the lender charges normal interest and a discount offsets it, so the customer sees no interest line.
- GST on interest and a processing fee are often cited as charges that are not offset. Varies by lender.
- Choosing EMI can mean giving up a cash or upfront discount.
- A 2013 RBI circular reportedly said zero percent interest is non-existent. Not verified at source.
