# Research: No-cost EMI hidden cost (2026-10-25)

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost EMI" is not always a free decision (topic bank #1).

## Why selected
Common audience confusion, expensive habit, empty publishing history, high save/share potential at festive-season checkout time.

## Primary sources consulted
None reachable. Direct fetch of primary and publisher sites was blocked or returned only search summaries. RBI documents were identified via search but not read:

## Research notes (secondary, via search summaries)
- Business Today, 2025-03-06 and 2025-12-06: cost is shifted, not removed (discount adjustment, merchant subvention, fees).
- Paisabazaar, BankBazaar, ClearTax: GST at 18% on processing fees; sources disagree on GST on the interest component.
- Press coverage of a September 2013 RBI circular says interest in zero-percent EMI schemes was often disguised as processing fee. Current status unconfirmed.

## Claims included in the carousel
