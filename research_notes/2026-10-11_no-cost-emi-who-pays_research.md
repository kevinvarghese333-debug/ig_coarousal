# Research: No-cost EMI, who pays the cost?

## Topic selected
No-cost (zero-cost) EMI: the price of the offer shows up in the cash price, fees or behaviour, not necessarily as a line called interest.

## Why it was selected
- Topic bank priority #1; nothing published or drafted in the previous 30 days (published_log.csv was empty).
- Festive sale season makes it timely (no specific date claim is made on slides).
- Common audience confusion with a clear mechanism and an easy decision rule.

## Research notes
- Per the RBI snippet: banks were told to refrain from low or zero percent interest schemes on consumer durable loans achieved by adjusting the discount from manufacturers or dealers, citing lack of transparency and distorted loan pricing. This supports the mechanism "interest is still there, the seller's discount offsets it". Current applicability to today's card and loan-app EMIs is not confirmed.
- Explainers consistently describe: lender charges interest, seller/merchant discount offsets it, and no-cost EMI often cannot be combined with an instant cash or card discount.
