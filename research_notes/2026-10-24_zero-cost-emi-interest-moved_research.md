# Research: Zero-cost EMI (2026-10-24)

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost" EMI is not free: where the interest goes.

## Why it was selected
Topic bank priority 1, nothing in the log (first run), common audience confusion, and timely during the October festive sale season.

## Primary sources consulted
None could be opened. rbi.org.in and capitalmind.in were blocked by the environment network proxy (403 / DNS failure). Only web-search excerpts were available.

## Research notes
- Search excerpts agree: the lender still charges interest; the seller or brand funds an upfront discount that offsets it; GST on the interest and a processing fee may remain; a bank discount and no-cost EMI are often mutually exclusive.
- RBI Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022 reportedly require principal, interest and upfront discount to be disclosed before EMI conversion and bar camouflaging interest as zero-interest. Not opened. [VERIFY]
- Older RBI guidance (2013) reportedly said zero-percent EMI schemes can hide interest in processing fees. [VERIFY]
