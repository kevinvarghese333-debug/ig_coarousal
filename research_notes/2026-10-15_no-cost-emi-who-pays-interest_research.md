# Research: No-Cost EMI, Who Pays the Interest?

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost" EMI is rarely free (priority topic 1 in the topic bank).

## Why it was selected
First run, no history. Common audience confusion, expensive everyday decision, evergreen, festive-season relevant. No topic published in the last 30 days.

## Primary sources consulted
The RBI website was not reachable from the run environment (DNS failure), so no primary source could be opened directly. Only search summaries of secondary reporting were available.

## Research notes
- Search summaries cite an RBI circular of September 2013 saying zero percent interest does not truly exist and that processing charge and rate should be uniform across products. Source: Business Standard report, https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html (2013). [VERIFY] (page could not be opened). Not used on slides.
- Reports of the RBI Master Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022 (dated 21 Apr 2022, effective 1 Jul 2022) quote: issuers shall ensure complete transparency in EMI conversion by clearly indicating the principal, interest and upfront discount provided by the merchant or issuer (to make it no cost), prior to conversion, and show it separately in the statement.
