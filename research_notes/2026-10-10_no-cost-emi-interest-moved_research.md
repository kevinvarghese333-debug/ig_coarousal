Date: 2026-10-10

## Research notes
- Mechanism: bank computes interest; merchant or brand gives an upfront discount roughly equal to it; buyer pays sticker price in instalments.
- GST on the interest component is charged to the buyer; commonly cited at 18%.
- Processing fees can apply on some offers.
- Cash discounts may not be available if the buyer picks the no-cost EMI.
- Current status of the 2013 RBI circular was not confirmed, so it is not used on slides.
