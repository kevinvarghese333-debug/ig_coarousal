# Research: No-cost EMI (2026-10-12)

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost" EMI is not always a free decision.

## Why it was selected
First run, no history. Top of the topic bank, a common audience confusion, strong save/share/comment potential. No date-sensitive development could be verified, so this is an evergreen mechanism piece.

## Primary sources consulted
None could be opened. rbi.org.in and other domains returned DNS failure from the environment. Only web-search snippets were available.

## Research notes (search snippets and secondary reporting only, so all [VERIFY])
- Reports describe a 17 Sep 2013 RBI circular saying interest in zero-percent EMI schemes is often camouflaged as a processing fee. [VERIFY] https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html (2013)
- RBI card directions reportedly require disclosure of principal, interest and upfront discount before EMI conversion, and bar presenting an interest-bearing EMI as zero-interest or no-cost. [VERIFY] https://www.rbi.org.in (2022, amended 2024; 2025 restatement unconfirmed)
- Key Fact Statement for retail loans reported effective 1 Oct 2024; scope for credit cards unclear. [VERIFY] https://www.rbi.org.in
