# Research Notes: The Loan App Cooling-Off Window

## Topic selected

How digital loan apps are built to make a small, fast loan feel harmless,
and the RBI disclosure and cooling-off rules that exist precisely because
that speed hides the real cost from the borrower.

## Why it was selected

- No topic has been published or drafted in the last 30 days (published_log.csv
  and carousel_topic_bank.md "Published" section are both empty).
- Matches priority order item 3 ("a common and expensive money mistake") and
  item 2 ("a common audience confusion") from the production workflow.
- Fits the Trap or Warning carousel type: appealing surface, missing question,
  risk, safer way to think.
- Strong, primary-source-backed regulatory mechanism (RBI Key Fact Statement,
  cooling-off period, FLDG cap) gives the carousel a concrete, defensible
  "hidden mechanism" instead of a vague warning. Low fabrication risk because
  the core facts are RBI rules, not company-specific figures.
- High save/share potential: a checklist people can act on before their next
  loan-app notification.

## Primary sources consulted

1. RBI Circular RBI/2024-25/18, DOR.STR.REC.13/13.03.00/2024-25, "Key Facts
   Statement (KFS) for Loans & Advances," April 15, 2024.
   https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/CIRCULARKFS1504242AE2500BAF494C2A82442B0B642705C1.PDF
   - Mandates a standardised Key Fact Statement before loan execution.
   - KFS must include the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), the recovery
     procedure, and grievance redressal officer details for digital
     lending/fintech loans.

2. RBI, "Guidelines on Digital Lending" (original digital lending
   guidelines, 2022), as summarised and cross-checked via RBI's own KFS
   circular and secondary legal analysis (Argus Partners, Kinara Capital).
   https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/GUIDELINESDIGITALLENDINGD5C35A71D8124A0E92AEB940A7D25BB3.PDF
   - Cooling-off / look-up period: not less than 3 days for loans with
     tenor of 7 days or more; not less than 1 day for loans with tenor
     under 7 days.
   - During this window, the borrower can exit the loan by paying the
     principal plus the proportionate APR, with no penalty.

3. RBI, "Guidelines on Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) in Digital Lending"
   (First Loss Default Guarantee / FLDG), June 8, 2023.
   https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/English/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3592
   - Total DLG/FLDG cover on an outstanding loan portfolio is capped at
     5% of that portfolio for RBI-regulated entities.
   - Cross-checked against secondary analysis (Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas,
     Business Standard/CRISIL commentary, June 2023) for context only, not
     for the core figure.

## Research notes

- The KFS and cooling-off rules exist specifically because the digital
  lending journey (a few taps, instant disbursal) was found to obscure the
  true cost and terms of the loan. This is the mechanism the carousel is
  built around.
- The cooling-off window is a real, usable consumer right: exit within the
  window and pay only principal plus proportionate interest, no penalty.
  Most borrowers are not aware this window exists, which is the "overlooked
  insight" of the carousel, not a claim that any specific app hides it.
- FLDG cap (5%) is used only as context for why speed and volume are
  structurally incentivised in this lending model. It is not used to make
  a claim about any specific lender's behaviour or numbers.
- No specific company, app name, interest rate example, or loan amount is
  attributed to any real fintech lender or NBFC in this carousel. All
  figures used on-slide are the regulatory figures above (3 days / 1 day
  cooling-off, 5% FLDG cap), each tied to its primary source.

## Claims included in the carousel (all with primary source, on-slide or in fine print)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| RBI requires a Key Fact Statement disclosing the all-in APR before a digital loan is signed | RBI Circular RBI/2024-25/18, April 15, 2024 |
| Cooling-off/look-up period: minimum 3 days for loans of 7+ days tenor, minimum 1 day for loans under 7 days tenor | RBI Digital Lending Guidelines, 2022 |
