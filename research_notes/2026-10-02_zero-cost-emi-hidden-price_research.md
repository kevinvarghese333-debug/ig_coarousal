## Research notes
- Secondary reports say the 2013 RBI circular stated that in zero percent EMI schemes on card outstanding, the interest element was often camouflaged and passed on as a processing fee, and that the concept of zero percent interest is non-existent.
- Reports of the time describe it as barring zero percent schemes on card purchases of consumer goods. "No-cost EMI" offers continue in the market, so the current regulatory position is NOT stated on slides. [VERIFY]
- Slides 5 and 6 are an illustrative calculation, not sourced data: cash price Rs 57,000, EMI 6 x Rs 10,000 = Rs 60,000. Solving 57,000 = sum of 10,000/(1+r)^k for k=1..6 gives r = 1.49% a month, about 17.8% a year simple annualised (19.4% effective). Slide says "about 18%".
