## Research notes
- Reported 2022 direction text: issuers must show principal, interest and upfront discount before conversion and in the statement; interest-bearing EMI should not be camouflaged as zero-interest/no-cost.
- Reported practice: seller/issuer discount offsets the interest; 18% GST on interest and fees reportedly remains with the customer.
- A 2025 direction set may supersede the 2022 text for commercial banks; not confirmed.
