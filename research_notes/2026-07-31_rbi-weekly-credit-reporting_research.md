# Research Notes: RBI Weekly Credit Reporting

## Topic Selected

RBI's shift from fortnightly to weekly credit bureau reporting for loans and credit cards, with the amended directions taking effect July 1, 2026, and what this means for people who treat a due date as a soft deadline.

## Why It Was Selected

- Priority 1 in the production workflow is a timely, verifiable Indian finance or business development. This rule change is dated for the current month (July 2026) and is directly relevant to the page's core audience: salaried professionals managing EMIs, credit cards and credit scores.
- It is also a common audience confusion in disguise: most people do not realise the reporting cadence to credit bureaus has changed twice in under two years (monthly, then fortnightly in 2024, now weekly from mid-2026).
- Nothing has been published on this account yet (published_log.csv is empty, carousel_topic_bank.md has no items in the Published section), so there is no 30-day repeat conflict.
- The topic bank's existing entries (zero-cost EMI, credit score/lender information, loan apps) are related themes but this is a materially different angle: a regulatory reporting-cadence change, not a product mechanic.

## Timeline Established (cross-source consensus)

1. Historical baseline: banks and NBFCs reported borrower data to credit information companies (CICs) once a month.
2. August 2024: RBI shortened this to once every fortnight (15 days), with the new norms required to take effect no later than January 1, 2025.
3. RBI subsequently mandated a further move to weekly reporting. The originally announced effective date was April 1, 2026.
4. This weekly-reporting effective date was later deferred to July 1, 2026, per RBI's own notification (as reported by Business Standard/CAalley).

Note of caution: sources differ slightly on the exact within-month reporting-day schedule (some cite 7th/14th/21st/28th/last day of month, others cite 9th/16th/23rd/last day of month). This inconsistency across secondary sources is the reason the exact reporting-day schedule was left out of on-slide copy and is flagged as [VERIFY].

## Claims Included in the Carousel

- Reporting frequency moved from monthly, to fortnightly (2024), to weekly (2026). Included with year markers only (no exact day-of-month schedule), since this is the consistent, corroborated part of the story across sources.
- The practical consumer effect: a late payment can reach a credit file faster than before (framed as "within about a week" rather than a precise guarantee), and conversely a cleared due can also reflect faster.
- No specific bank, NBFC, fintech app, or credit bureau is named, keeping the carousel free of product endorsement or criticism of a named entity.
