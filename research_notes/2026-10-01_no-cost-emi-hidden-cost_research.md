# Research: No-cost EMI hides a cost

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost EMI" is not always a free decision (topic bank priority #1).

## Why it was selected
Topic bank and log were empty, so no repeat risk. Common audience confusion, expensive habit, evergreen, and RBI has a clear, checkable rule on it.

## Primary sources consulted
- RBI Master Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card, Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022 (issued 21 Apr 2022, effective 1 Jul 2022): https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12300. The rbi.org.in domain was blocked by this environment's proxy, so the wording was confirmed only through secondary summaries (Mondaq, LinkedIn synopsis, search summaries). [VERIFY]

## Research notes
- Direction text as summarised: issuers must indicate principal, interest and upfront discount provided by merchant/issuer (to make it no cost) before conversion, and show the same separately on the statement. EMI conversion with an interest component shall not be camouflaged as zero-interest/no-cost EMI.
- Consumer explainers say cost can also appear as processing fees and GST on interest. Not used on slides because rates and fees vary. [VERIFY]
- Example numbers are hypothetical and labelled illustrative.
