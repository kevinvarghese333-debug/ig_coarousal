## Research notes
- Mechanism widely described: lender charges interest, seller gives a discount of about the same size, invoice shows no interest.
- RBI language reported: concept of zero percent interest is non-existent; interest is often camouflaged as processing fee (2013 notice, [VERIFY]).
- Current rule reported: EMI conversion with an interest component shall not be camouflaged as zero-interest or no-cost EMI (Para 6(b)(iii), 2022 Master Direction) [VERIFY].
