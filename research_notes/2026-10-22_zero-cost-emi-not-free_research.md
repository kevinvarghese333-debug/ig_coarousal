## Research notes
- Per search summaries, RBI in 2013 told banks that zero-percent interest is a concept that does not really exist and that interest was being camouflaged as processing fee.
- Common structure: interest offered as an upfront discount, or interest added to the product price and repaid in EMIs; merchant or brand often bears the discount.
- Blogs state GST at 18% applies to interest and processing fee on card EMI conversions.
- Buyers may lose an instant discount that applies to full upfront payment.

## Claims included in the carousel
