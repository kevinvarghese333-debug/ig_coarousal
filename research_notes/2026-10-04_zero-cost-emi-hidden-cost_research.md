## Research notes
- RBI's 2013 circular, as quoted in secondary coverage: interest in zero-percent card EMI schemes was often camouflaged as a processing fee, and the concept of zero-percent interest is non-existent.
- Secondary reporting says the 2022 card Master Direction says an EMI with an interest component should not be camouflaged as zero-interest or no-cost EMI. [VERIFY]
- Capitalmind explains the mechanism: the merchant or manufacturer gives an "EMI discount" that is lost if you pay in full, and processing fees may carry GST.
- The carousel example uses invented round numbers for clarity and labels them illustrative. Implied rate computed by solving PV of 6 x ₹5,000 = ₹28,500: 1.49% a month, about 17.8% a year nominal.
