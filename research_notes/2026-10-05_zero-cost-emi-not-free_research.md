Why "no-cost EMI" is not automatically free.

## Research notes
- Secondary reporting (BusinessToday 2025-03-06, search summaries of RBI's 2022 credit card Master Direction) says: issuers must show principal, interest and upfront discount before EMI conversion and on the statement; an interest component must not be camouflaged as zero-interest/no-cost.
- Reported mechanisms: merchant discount equal to interest, or interest waived with a processing fee.
- An older RBI circular (2013) is reported to have criticised zero-percent schemes. Not used on slides.
