## Research notes

- 2013: RBI asked banks to keep interest rates and processing fees uniform on card EMI schemes for retail products and discouraged zero-percent schemes. Reports quote RBI as saying the concept of zero percent interest is non-existent and that interest was often camouflaged as processing fees. RBI also said the loan amount should be sanctioned after taking the discount into account.
- Mechanism used on slides: the lender still earns a return. The cost is often met by a seller or brand discount, or sits in a price, fee or lost cash discount. Framed with "often" and "sometimes".
- Slide 5 is an arithmetic illustration, not sourced data: ₹57,000 cash price versus ₹60,000 in 6 instalments of ₹10,000, so ₹3,000 gap.
