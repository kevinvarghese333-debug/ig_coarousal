# Research: "No-cost" EMI is not a free decision

## Topic selected
Why "no-cost" EMI is not always a free decision (Topic bank priority 1).

## Why it was selected
Topic bank priority 1, never published, and timely: it is October, the festive-sale season, when no-cost EMI is promoted heavily. Common audience confusion with a clear mechanism.

## Research notes (secondary summaries only)
- RBI Master Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct, issued 21 Apr 2022, effective 1 Jul 2022: reportedly says EMI conversion with an interest component should not be camouflaged as zero-interest or no-cost. Source: https://www.livelaw.in/law-firms/law-firm-articles-/issuance-and-conduct-directions-2022-rbi-urban-cooperative-banks-pradhan-mantri-jan-dhan-yojana-non-banking-financial-companies-204696 and https://www.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1214312/master-directions-on-credit-card-and-debit-card-issuance-and-conduct-directions-2022
- RBI Sept 2013 circular: reportedly said banks hid interest in processing fees on zero-percent schemes. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html (25 Sep 2013)
- Disclosure of principal, interest and upfront discount: https://www.sahi.com/blogs/is-no-cost-emi-really-free-things-to-check-before-buying (undated blog)
