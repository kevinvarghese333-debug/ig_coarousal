## Claims deliberately excluded from on-slide copy

- Any single precise UPI digital-gold transaction count or rupee value,
  since sources disagreed slightly on exact figures for the same period.
- Any claim naming a specific vault provider (MMTC-PAMP, SafeGold,
  Augmont) as currently failing to back its gold holdings. No evidence of
  this was found; the on-slide point is about the absence of a legal audit
  requirement, not an accusation against any named company.
- Any specific GST rate or purchase cap figure, not independently
  verified this session.
