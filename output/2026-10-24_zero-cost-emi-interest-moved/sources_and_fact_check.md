Primary sources (rbi.org.in, capitalmind.in) could not be opened from the production environment (network proxy blocked them). Regulatory points below come from search-result excerpts only and are marked [VERIFY]. None of them appears on a slide as an attributed RBI claim.

## On-slide numbers (our own arithmetic, illustrative)
- Assumption: ₹30,000 phone, 6 monthly EMIs, 15% a year, reducing balance, monthly rate 1.25%.
- EMI = ₹5,221; total paid = ₹31,326; interest = ₹1,326; seller discount assumed equal to interest; 18% GST on interest = ₹239 (rounded).
- Slides label this "illustrative" and state the assumptions. Real offers differ by lender and seller. [VERIFY] that 18% GST applies to the interest and processing fee on card EMI: CBIC / GST Council rate schedule for financial services.

## Mechanism claim
- Seller or brand funds a discount that roughly offsets EMI interest; bank still charges interest. Source: secondary explainers (Capitalmind "How a zero-cost EMI actually costs 9%", https://www.capitalmind.in/insights/how-a-zero-cost-emi-actually-costs-9 ; Sahi, BasuNivesh, CardExpress).
