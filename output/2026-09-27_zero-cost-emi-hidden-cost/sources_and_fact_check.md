# Sources and Fact-Check: Zero-Cost EMI Is Not a Free Decision

## Claims used on-slide and their support

1. **"No-cost EMI" schemes generally work by the lender still charging interest internally, with a merchant or brand paying that interest upfront (interest subvention) instead of the interest being waived outright.** (Slide 4)
   - Forbes India: https://www.forbesindia.com/article/news/deep-dive/how-no-cost-emis-have-changed-the-way-indians-shop/2998406/1
   - Moneyview: https://moneyview.in/insights/what-is-the-meaning-of-no-cost-emi-and-how-does-it-works
   - Zerodha Varsity: https://zerodhavarsity.substack.com/p/the-actual-cost-of-no-cost-emi

2. **The buyer who chooses "no-cost" EMI typically forgoes the cash discount a full-cash buyer would get on the same product.** (Slides 5-6)
   - Forbes India (as above)
   - Moneyview (as above)
   - The ₹38,000 / ₹40,000 / ₹2,000 figures are an illustrative example only, not tied to any real product, lender or current offer.

3. **In 2013, the RBI pushed back on "0%"-branded EMI schemes because the interest cost was being camouflaged rather than removed.** (Slide 7) [VERIFY before publishing]
   - Business Standard (25 Sept 2013): https://www.business-standard.com/article/reuters/rbi-asks-banks-to-charge-uniform-interest-rate-on-retail-emi-schemes-113092500642_1.html
   - Business Standard (25 Sept 2013): https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html
   - Moneylife: https://www.moneylife.in/article/rbi-says-no-zero-percent-interest-scheme-for-buying-consumer-goods/34611.html
   - Status: corroborated by three independent 2013 press reports; the primary RBI circular (number and exact date) was not retrieved directly from rbi.org.in in this research session. On-slide copy avoids quoting or citing a specific circular number for this reason.
