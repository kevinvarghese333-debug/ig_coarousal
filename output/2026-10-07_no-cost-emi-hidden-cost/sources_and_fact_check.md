# Sources and Fact Check: No-cost EMI

Example figures on slides 2, 5 and 6 are **illustrative, self-calculated numbers**, not a real offer. Slide 6 "about 18% a year" is our own IRR calculation: borrow ₹57,000, repay 6 x ₹10,000 monthly (monthly rate ~1.49%, nominal ~17.8% a year), excluding fees and GST.

## [VERIFY] items (none of these are stated as fact on the slides)
1. RBI wording that an EMI with an interest component should not be advertised as "zero-interest" or "no-cost". Source needed: RBI Credit Card and Debit Card Master Direction 2022 and its 2025 restated directions. Primary site was blocked in this environment.
2. RBI requirement to show principal, interest and discount separately on the statement. Same source.
3. GST applying on interest and processing fees for card EMI conversion. Source: issuer terms / GST rules.
4. That merchants may remove a cash discount on no-cost EMI. Practices vary; slides use "can".
