# "No-cost EMI" still costs something

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: "No-cost EMI" is a label, not a guarantee. The cost is often moved somewhere you are not looking.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried viewers, 21 to 40, buying phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: A small monthly number feels harmless.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, plus comments (discount or EMI)
- Why people may save, share or comment: Three-question checkout rule; relatable purchase; a real either/or choice.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity: Where does the interest go on a no-cost EMI?
2. Contrarian: "No-cost EMI" still costs something.
3. Relatable-pain: ₹10,000 a month felt easy. The total did not.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism: The interest did not vanish. It moved.
5. Warning-or-risk: Check this before you tap EMI.

**Selected hook:** "No-cost EMI" still costs something.

**Why it wins:** It corrects a belief the viewer holds at every checkout, in six words.
