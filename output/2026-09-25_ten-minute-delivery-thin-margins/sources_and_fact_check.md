## Claims excluded from slides

- No forward-looking claim about whether Blinkit, Zepto or any quick-commerce
  company will become durably profitable.
- No investment recommendation, buy/sell call, or comparison framed as advice.
- No per-order profit/loss figure stated to the exact rupee; all figures use
  "about."
- Store counts, order volumes and market-share percentages beyond what the
  core idea needed were left out of the slides to keep to one idea.
