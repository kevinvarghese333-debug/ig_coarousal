Blinkit just had its best quarter ever. It kept about 26 paise for every 100 rupees that moved through it.

That is not a typo, and it is not a failure either. It is the first time the business has been profitable at this scale, according to its parent company's latest results. The margin is just razor thin, because running 10-minute delivery means paying for a dark store near you and a rider on standby whether or not you order tonight.

A rival, Zepto, is growing even faster, and its yearly loss grew past ₹5,900 crore in the same period. Both companies are winning the growth race. Neither has fully won the profit race yet.

This is not a call to buy, sell or avoid anything. It is a reminder that a growing app and a healthy business are two different questions, and headlines usually answer only one of them.

Do you think 10-minute delivery becomes a genuinely profitable business, or a permanently subsidised convenience?
