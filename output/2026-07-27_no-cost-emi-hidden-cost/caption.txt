"No Cost EMI" is one of the most trusted badges at checkout, and one of the least explained. In 2013, RBI told banks plainly that a truly free EMI does not exist. That does not mean the schemes are illegal or always bad. It means the cost has to move somewhere else: into the listed price, into a processing fee, or into a cash discount you no longer receive.

None of this makes EMI wrong. It makes it a decision, not a default. The part worth noticing is not the rupee amount. It is the moment No Cost EMI quietly removes: the pause where you ask whether you can actually afford the thing today, in full.
