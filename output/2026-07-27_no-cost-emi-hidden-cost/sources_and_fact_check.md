## [VERIFY] before publishing

1. The RBI claims above are sourced from contemporaneous news coverage of a September 2013 RBI communication to banks, not the original RBI circular/press release text. Locate the primary RBI document (rbi.org.in) if a harder citation is wanted.
2. Confirm the current GST treatment of EMI/loan processing fees at time of publishing; no specific percentage is stated on-slide, so this only matters if a number is added later (e.g. in comments or caption edits).
3. Confirm whether the "discount must be passed to EMI buyers" principle is being consistently followed by lenders in practice today; the carousel describes the 2013 regulatory principle, not a live audit of current lender behaviour.
