# Sources and Fact-Check: How Loan Apps Make Small Loans Feel Harmless

## Claims used on-slide and their support

1. **"Every digital loan must now carry a Key Fact Statement... the full yearly cost, in APR, before you accept." (Slide 4)**
   Basis: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines, effective 2 September 2022, require regulated entities and their lending service provider partners to issue a standardised Key Fact Statement (KFS) disclosing the All-inclusive Cost as an Annual Percentage Rate (APR) before the loan agreement is signed.
   Status: Supported by multiple independent secondary summaries. [VERIFY] against the primary RBI circular before publishing, since `rbi.org.in` and `rbidocs.rbi.org.in` could not be reached directly from this environment.

2. **"Missed repayment still reaches your credit report, and recovery calls still happen, once the short cooling-off window closes." (Slide 7)**
   Basis: RBI's digital lending framework includes a borrower cooling-off/look-up period after which normal repayment and recovery obligations apply; standard credit bureau reporting practice applies to regulated loans generally.
   Status: Directionally supported. The exact cooling-off day count was deliberately not stated on-slide because secondary sources vary by loan tenure and the primary circular could not be confirmed. [VERIFY] exact day counts if a future carousel wants to state them.

3. **"A small, frequent loan... more loans, more repayments, more data, more cross-selling, all faster than one large loan could ever produce." (Slide 6)**
   Basis: Reasoning drawn from the mechanics of short-tenure digital lending and RBI's Guidelines on Default Loss Guarantee (DLG/FLDG) in Digital Lending, issued 8 June 2023, which cap risk-sharing arrangements between lenders and lending service providers at 5 percent of a loan portfolio, a structure that rewards portfolio velocity.
   Status: Framed as reasoning, not as a cited statistic. The 5 percent cap figure itself was not used on-slide. [VERIFY] if used directly in a future carousel.

4. **The ₹3,000 / ₹5,000 / ₹8,000 repeat-borrowing sequence (Slide 5)**
   Basis: None. Explicitly labelled on-slide as "a pattern many borrowers describe, not one specific case." This is an illustrative composite, not real borrower data, and must not be read as a verified statistic.
   Status: Illustrative only, not a factual claim requiring a source.
