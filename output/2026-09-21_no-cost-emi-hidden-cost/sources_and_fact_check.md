# Sources and Fact Check: Zero-Cost EMI

Full research is in
`research_notes/2026-09-21_no-cost-emi-hidden-cost_research.md`. This file
is the condensed, slide-facing version.

## Claims used on slides and their support

| Claim (as used on-slide) | Support | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Banks are not built to lend at zero percent; the interest is recovered elsewhere | RBI's 2013 position on camouflaged zero percent interest schemes, corroborated by 3 independent secondary sources | Corroborated, primary RBI PDF not directly fetched this run — [VERIFY] before publishing |
| A cash-price discount often disappears once a buyer chooses no-cost EMI | 3+ independent 2026 secondary explainers describing current bank/merchant practice | Corroborated by secondary sources |
| GST can apply to the interest component the bank calculates internally, even when a merchant discount offsets it | 2 independent 2026 secondary sources (Kotak Mutual Fund, CardCheck) | Corroborated by secondary sources |
| A processing fee is commonly charged to convert a purchase into EMI | Multiple sources confirm the fee exists; amounts vary widely by bank | Existence corroborated; no rupee figure used on-slide |
