"No-cost EMI" is a pricing label, not a law of nature.

A loan always has a cost. When the EMI looks free, that cost usually sits somewhere else: a cash discount you give up by choosing EMI, a processing fee, or other charges that the bank or merchant applies.

The simple check is to compare two numbers: the price if you pay in full today, and the total you will pay across all the instalments. If the second number is bigger, you are borrowing, whatever the banner says.

The example in this carousel uses illustrative numbers to show the arithmetic. Real offers differ by merchant, card and lender, so check the current terms on your own checkout screen and card statement.

There is also a quieter cost. A small monthly amount makes a big purchase feel affordable. That is useful for a merchant. It is not always useful for you.
