# Where does the interest on a "no-cost" EMI go?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost / zero-cost EMI
- Central idea: A "no-cost" EMI usually moves the cost somewhere else; it does not remove it.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried viewers, 21 to 40, who buy phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: It feels free, so people stop checking the cash price and their own motives.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves (three-question checklist) and comments (cash price vs EMI)
- Why people may save, share or comment: Checkout-ready rule of three questions; a relatable moment; a real judgement question.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: Where does the interest on a "no-cost" EMI go?
2. Contrarian hook: "No-cost" EMI is not a free decision.
3. Relatable-pain hook: The EMI is small. The habit is not.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: How a seller pays your EMI interest for you
5. Warning-or-risk hook: Check this before you tap "no-cost EMI"

**Selected hook:** Where does the interest on a "no-cost" EMI go?

**Why it wins:** It is a question the viewer has never asked about a screen they use, so the answer has to be on the next swipe.
