# Someone Is Paying for "No-Cost" EMI

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: "No-cost" EMI does not remove the cost of credit. It moves it somewhere you may not look.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried buyers, 21 to 40, buying phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: It says no-cost, so why does it feel like I am missing something?
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, with comments as secondary
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question checkout checklist, a friend-forward warning, and a question about misleading "zero" offers.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: Who pays for "no-cost" EMI?
2. Contrarian hook: "No-cost" EMI is not free.
3. Relatable-pain hook: You tapped "no-cost EMI". Did you check the price?
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: The interest did not disappear. It moved.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: Zero interest is not zero cost.

**Selected hook:** Someone is paying for "no-cost". It may be you.

**Why it wins:** It names a familiar checkout label and creates tension with a single, specific doubt.
