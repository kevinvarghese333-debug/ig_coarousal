Festive sale season is when "no-cost EMI" shows up on every product page.

Here is the part worth knowing. In most cases the lender is still charging interest. What changes is who appears to pay it. Often the seller adjusts the price with an upfront discount that cancels the interest, so the headline interest figure looks like zero.

What can still reach you: GST on the interest component, a processing fee, and charges if you close the loan early. Terms differ by lender, card and seller, so check the current terms on the checkout screen or the loan summary before you confirm.

The example in the carousel is illustrative. It assumes a ₹60,000 phone, 6 months and 15% a year. Your numbers will differ.

One habit that helps: ask for the price if you pay upfront today, and compare that number with the EMI price. They are not always the same.
