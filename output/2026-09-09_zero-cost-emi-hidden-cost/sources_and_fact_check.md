# Sources and Fact Check: Zero-Cost EMI Is Not a Free Decision

## Claims made on-slide and their support

1. **"Retailers often price 'no-cost EMI' without the cash discount a full-payment buyer gets."** (Slide 4)
   General mechanism, consistently described across multiple independent consumer-finance explainers (moneyview.in, getonecard.app, herofincorp.com, gripinvest.in). Treated as a safe general description, not a single-source statistic.

2. **Full-payment vs. no-cost-EMI price comparison (Rs 40,500 vs Rs 45,000, Rs 4,500 difference).** (Slide 5)
   Clearly labelled on the slide as "Illustrative example." This is a rounded, generic worked example used to demonstrate the mechanism above, not a real transaction, retailer or lender.

3. **RBI has flagged the camouflaging of interest as a processing fee in zero-percent EMI schemes since 2013.** (Caption only, phrased in general terms; not stated on any slide with a specific notification number)
   - Reported via secondary sources as RBI notification RBI/2013-14/292, DBS.CO.PPD.No.3578/11.01.005/2013-14.
   - Sources: Moneylife (https://www.moneylife.in/article/rbi-says-no-zero-percent-interest-scheme-for-buying-consumer-goods/34611.html), Business Standard (https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html), Onmanorama (https://www.onmanorama.com/news/business/2023/02/04/zero-percent-interest-schemes-banned-rbi.html).
   - **[VERIFY]** Direct access to rbi.org.in was blocked in this environment, so the exact notification number and date (reported variously as 17 or 20 September 2013) could not be independently confirmed against the primary source. Confirm on rbi.org.in before publishing, or soften/remove the RBI reference if it cannot be verified in time.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- A specific processing-fee percentage (one source cited "2 to 4% of purchase value plus GST") was excluded from all slide copy and the caption because it varies by issuer and was sourced from a single non-primary explainer.
- Any claim tying no-cost EMI to specific smartphone-market growth figures was excluded; found in only one secondary blog with no primary corroboration available in this environment.
