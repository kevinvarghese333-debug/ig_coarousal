Festive-sale season is when "No-cost EMI" shows up on every checkout screen.

The EMI is easy to see. The cost is harder to see.

Here is the idea. A lender normally earns interest on an instalment loan. In a "no-cost" offer, a discount can offset that interest, so the monthly payments add up to the sticker price. The interest has not disappeared. It has been moved somewhere else: a lost cash discount, a processing fee, or a listed price that was never the lowest.

The numbers in this carousel are illustrative, not a real offer. Real offers vary by merchant, card and lender, so read the terms of the one in front of you.

The simple test is to ask for the pay-in-full price and compare it with the total you will pay on EMI. If the gap is zero, good. If it is not, now you know what you are paying for.
