## Illustrative (not sourced) figures

Slides 4 and 5 use a hypothetical ₹50,000 phone with a ₹2,500 discount/interest gap to explain the mechanism. These are Kevin's own round numbers for teaching, explicitly labelled "A SIMPLE EXAMPLE" and "ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE" on-slide, and are not a reported real transaction. ## Claims deliberately excluded

- A single-source Upstox rupee example (₹1,270 foregone discount on a ₹30,000 item; ₹3,000 markup plus ₹200 fee on a ₹47,000 TV) was not presented as a real reported figure.
- A Business Standard claim of "~50% year-on-year rise in EMI e-commerce usage" for the 2025 festive season was excluded; its base period and methodology could not be confirmed.
- A December 2023 Amazon-sourced "3 out of 4 products via no-cost EMI" statistic was excluded as outside the 12-month recency window.
- No specific universal processing-fee number was used, since fees vary meaningfully by lender, card and merchant.
- An AI-search paraphrase suggesting a recent/2025-2026 RBI statement specifically about "no-cost EMI" was excluded as unverified and likely a conflation with the 2013 action.
