That "₹0 extra cost" line at checkout is doing a lot of work. No-cost EMI is not free credit. In most cases, the interest you are not paying is funded by a cash or instant discount you quietly give up, so the final price ends up roughly the same either way.

The bigger cost is not even the money. Paying in full makes a price sting for one moment, and that sting is doing you a favour: it makes you pause. Split into ten quiet instalments, that moment disappears, and research on instalment payments has found people spend more once a cost feels smaller.

None of this means EMI is always wrong. It means the "no cost" label is doing marketing, not accounting. Before your next big purchase, ask what discount you are giving up, what the processing fee is, and whether you would still buy it at the full price today.

Would you still buy it if the discount was shown instead of hidden?
