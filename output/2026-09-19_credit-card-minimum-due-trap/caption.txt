Most people track one number on their credit card bill: the due date.
Miss it, and you expect trouble. Meet it, and you assume you're safe.

But the due date only tells you when to pay. It says nothing about how
much. Pay only the minimum, right on time, and the interest-free period on
that bill doesn't pause. It ends, for the balance you didn't clear and for
every new purchase you make until you pay the full amount again.

That's the part that catches people off guard. Not a missed payment. A
"safe" one that was never actually full.

This isn't about panicking over every minimum-due payment. Sometimes it's
the only option, and that's fine, if you treat what's left like the loan
it has become, not a bill you already handled.

Before your next statement lands, check one thing: are you paying the
total amount due, or just the minimum? What's stopped you from clearing
the full bill when you could?
