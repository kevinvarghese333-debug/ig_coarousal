## Claims researched and excluded from this carousel

- RBI's reported "3-day grace period" before a late fee or CIBIL "past
  due" reporting can apply, and its effective date. Search results gave
  conflicting effective dates for this rule and it could not be
  reconciled from summaries alone in this session, so it was left out of
  the carousel entirely rather than published unverified.
- Any specific bank name, card product, or exact interest rate. The
  carousel intentionally stays generic ("many cards") to avoid an
  unverifiable universal claim about a named lender.
