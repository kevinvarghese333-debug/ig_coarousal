# Sources and Fact-Check: Zero-Cost EMI Is Not Actually Zero

See `research_notes/2026-08-26_zero-cost-emi-hidden-cost_research.md` in the repository root for the full research file. Summary for quick reference before publishing:

## Claims used on slides

1. **Slide 4**: "RBI has said plainly: zero percent interest is not a real thing, only a repackaged cost."
   - Basis: RBI reportedly told banks in a 2013 circular (RBI/2013-14/292, DBS.CO.PPD No. 3578/11.01.005/2013-14, dated 17 Sept 2013) that "the very concept of zero percent interest is non-existent" and that the interest element is often camouflaged as a processing fee.
   - Status: **[VERIFY]**. Sourced from secondary reporting (Business Standard, Moneylife) only. Direct confirmation against rbi.org.in was attempted but the domain is blocked from this environment's network. Confirm circular number and exact wording before publishing.

2. **Slide 4/mechanism**: "no cost" EMI cost is typically funded through a forgone cash/instant discount and/or a processing fee.
   - Status: Widely and consistently reported across multiple independent sources (see research file). Phrased generally, not attributed to any single bank or product.

3. **Slide 5**: ₹40,000 phone, ₹38,000 upfront with cash discount, ₹499 + GST processing fee, ~₹2,590 real gap.
   - Status: **Illustrative example only**, invented for teaching the mechanism with round numbers. Explicitly labelled on-slide as "AN ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE, NOT A SPECIFIC PRODUCT." Not a real product, bank or transaction.

4. **General**: EMI processing fees commonly attract GST.
   - Status: **[VERIFY]** current rate before publishing. Draft avoids stating a specific GST percentage on-slide.

## [VERIFY] items to close before Instagram publishing

- Exact RBI circular number/date/wording (blocked source: rbi.org.in)
- Current GST rate applicable to EMI processing fees
- Whether "no cost EMI" remains the common market term used by major banks/platforms at time of posting

## Claims deliberately excluded from on-slide copy

- Specific bank-by-bank processing fee amounts (fees vary by bank/card/ticket size, not verified against official fee schedules)
- Reward-point forfeiture on EMI transactions (issuer-dependent, not universal)
- Any claim that RBI issued an outright "ban" on no-cost EMI (reporting is mixed between "ban" and "disclosure/uniformity requirement," draft uses the more cautious framing)
