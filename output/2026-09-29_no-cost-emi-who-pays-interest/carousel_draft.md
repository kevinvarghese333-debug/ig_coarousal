# "No-Cost" EMI: So Who Pays the Interest?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: "No-cost" EMI usually moves the interest into a discount, price or fees instead of removing it.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried buyers aged 21 to 40 who use card or store EMI at checkout
- Audience tension: The screen says ₹0 interest, but lenders do not lend for free
- Primary engagement goal: Saves and comments
- Why people may save, share or comment: The three-question checkout rule is saveable; the cash discount vs EMI question invites a real judgement

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: "No-cost" EMI. So who pays the interest?
2. Contrarian hook: Zero interest is not zero cost.
3. Relatable-pain hook: Your phone EMI looks small. Check what it replaced.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: How stores make interest disappear from the screen.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: The checkout line that hides the real price.

**Selected hook:** "No-cost" EMI. So who pays the interest?

**Why it wins:** It names a familiar checkout moment and asks a question the viewer cannot answer instantly.
