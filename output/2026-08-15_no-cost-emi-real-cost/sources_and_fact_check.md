# Sources and Fact-Check: "Zero-cost EMI has a cost. It is hidden, not removed."

## Claims used on-slide

1. **Slide 4:** "RBI has been clear since 2013: zero percent interest does
   not exist." Basis: RBI notification, "Zero percent Interest Finance
   Schemes for Consumer Durables," dated September 25, 2013, corroborated
   by five independent secondary sources (see below). The primary RBI PDF
   could not be fetched directly in this session due to network
   restrictions; the paraphrase is built from consistent secondary
   reporting, not a verbatim read of the circular. **[VERIFY before
   publishing: open the RBI link and confirm exact wording.]**
2. **Slide 4:** "The interest does not disappear. It moves into the
   product price, a lost cash discount, or a processing fee." Basis: the
   same RBI notification's stated rationale (interest camouflaged as
   processing fee or built into pricing) plus general, widely reported
   industry practice of merchants withdrawing instant/cash discounts on
   EMI conversion. Worded with "often," not as a universal rule.
3. **Slide 5:** ₹40,000 phone / ₹1,500 discount example. This is an
   explicit illustrative example, not a real transaction, retailer, or
   verified statistic, and is labelled as such on the slide itself.
