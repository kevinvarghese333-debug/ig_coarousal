## Claims deliberately excluded from on-slide copy

- Any single bank's exact current interest rate (e.g., a specific percentage tied to a named bank) — excluded because rates vary by card and change over time.
- A specific number of interest-free days (commonly cited as 20 to 50 days) — excluded because it varies by issuer and billing cycle.
- Any RBI text presented as a verbatim quotation — the mechanism is described in substance rather than quoted directly, since the primary PDF could not be re-fetched in this research session.
