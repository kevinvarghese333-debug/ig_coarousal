# Sources and Fact Check: The Minimum-Due Trap

## Central claim on-slide

Paying only the Minimum Amount Due (even before the due date) does not
preserve a credit card's interest-free period. Under the RBI Master
Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct (as amended,
effective 2024-03-07), if the Total Amount Due is not paid in full, the
card issuer may charge interest from the date of each transaction in that
cycle, on the outstanding amount, rather than only from the payment due
date forward.
