You probably have a system. Pay something before the due date, keep the account in good standing, move on. That system protects your credit score. It does not protect your wallet.

Under RBI's credit card rules, the interest-free period only survives if you clear the Total Amount Due in full. Pay only the Minimum Amount Due, and the card issuer can charge interest from the date of each purchase in that cycle, not from the due date, and not only on the part you did not pay.

This is not a trick any single bank invented. It is how revolving credit is built to work almost everywhere. The button that feels like the safe, responsible choice is often the most expensive one available to you.

Next time your statement lands, look for the Total Amount Due first. The minimum due is not a repayment plan. It is a floor.

Have you ever paid only the minimum and been surprised by the interest that showed up next month?
