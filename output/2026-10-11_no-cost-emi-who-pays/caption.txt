Festive sale season is when "no-cost EMI" shows up on every checkout screen.

Here is the part worth pausing on. In many such offers, the lender still earns interest. The seller either absorbs it or adjusts the price somewhere else. So the cost does not always vanish. It can move into a cash discount you do not get, or a fee you only notice later.

That is why the useful question is simple: what would I pay if I paid in full today? Put that number next to the EMI total. The gap, if there is one, is the real price of spreading the payment.

The numbers in this carousel are illustrative, not a real offer. Terms differ across lenders, cards and sellers, so check the current terms, including any processing fee, before you confirm.

EMIs can be sensible. The trap is letting a small monthly number decide for you.
