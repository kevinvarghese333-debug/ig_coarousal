That "no cost EMI" badge at checkout is not a discount. It is a different way of collecting the same cost.

Most no-cost EMI offers work by quietly withdrawing the cash discount you would have gotten for paying upfront, and sometimes adding a processing fee on top. RBI flagged this back in 2013, saying the very idea of zero percent interest does not exist in these schemes. The offer is not illegal. It is just named better than it works.

The fix is not avoiding EMI. It is comparing the right number. Not the monthly instalment. The total amount you actually pay, cash price versus EMI price, before you tap confirm.

Save this before your next big purchase and run the two totals side by side.
