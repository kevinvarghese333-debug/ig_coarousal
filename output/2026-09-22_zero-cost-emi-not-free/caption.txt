That "No Cost EMI" button at checkout is doing more work than it lets on. Banks do not lend for free, so when a screen shows zero percent interest, the interest has not disappeared. It has usually moved.

Sometimes it shows up as a cash discount you would have got for paying upfront, quietly removed the moment you choose EMI. Sometimes it shows up as a processing fee. Sometimes it is GST charged on interest the bank still records internally, even though your bill calls it "no cost."

RBI flagged this pattern back in 2013, calling the zero percent framing "camouflaged." The label changed from "zero percent" to "no cost." The mechanism mostly did not.

None of this means EMI is wrong for you. It means "no cost" is a marketing line, not a receipt. Before you tap that box, run the three-step check on slide 8 and see what the actual gap is.
