Loan apps did not get sneakier. Some of them got more regulated. Since May 2025, RBI requires every digital lender to hand you a Key Fact Statement, one document with the real annualised cost of your loan, before you accept. Most borrowers have never opened one.

Here is the part fewer people know: the app on your phone is often not the actual lender. It is a Lending Service Provider working for a bank or NBFC that RBI actually regulates. The rule was built to make that relationship, and the real cost, impossible to miss.

None of that changes the interface. One button. One pre-filled amount. A cooling-off window you are never reminded exists. The paperwork got honest. The design stayed exactly as easy as before.

This is not a reason to panic about every lending app. It is a reason to spend the same 90 seconds reading the number that you spent accepting the loan.
