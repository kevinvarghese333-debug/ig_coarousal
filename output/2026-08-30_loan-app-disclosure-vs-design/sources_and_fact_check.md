## Claims deliberately excluded from on-slide copy

- Any specific APR percentage or fee amount for a "typical" small loan app. APR varies by loan and lender; stating one would misrepresent individual products.
- An exact minimum day-count for the cooling-off period. Secondary sources agree a window exists and is set by each lender's board, but this run could not confirm the regulator-mandated floor against the primary RBI text.
- The exact date the RBI's consumer-facing directory of verified Digital Lending Apps went live. Sources disagreed (June 15, 2025 reporting deadline vs. a July 1, 2025 "operational" date), so no date is used on-slide.
- Any specific loan app, bank or NBFC by name. The carousel stays generic to the regulatory framework.
