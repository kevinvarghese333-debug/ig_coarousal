## Note on research method

Direct access to rbi.org.in and a taxguru.in mirror of the RBI Master
Direction was blocked by this session's network egress policy. All
RBI-sourced claims are based on web-search summaries that themselves quote
the Master Direction, not a direct read of the primary PDF. Kevin should
confirm the exact clause against the live RBI document before publishing.

No claim in this carousel gives personalised financial, credit or borrowing
advice, names a specific lender as good or bad, or promises a guaranteed
outcome. All rate and fee figures are presented as ranges with "roughly" /
"often" / "can," per the brand's financial-accuracy rules.
