## Claims deliberately excluded from on-slide copy

- Any specific bank name, lender, product, interest rate, processing fee, or rupee figure. No verified, current, product-specific number was available, so the carousel stays at the mechanism level (per the "no invented statistics" and "avoid universal claims about lenders" rules).
- A commonly cited "4 to 8 percent" gap between no-cost EMI and discounted cash price. This figure appears in aggregator content but is not primary-sourced, so it was left out.
- Any claim that GST is charged on the waived-interest component "in most" no-cost EMI schemes. This varies by lender and is not uniformly confirmed.
- Any claim about whether RBI's 2013 position reflects current, active 2026 enforcement. The carousel frames the reference as historical regulatory context only.
