## Claims used on-slide and their sources

| Claim (slide) | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Lenders must give a Key Fact Statement (KFS) showing the real APR before a digital loan agreement (Slide 7, 8) | RBI, Guidelines on Digital Lending | 2 September 2022 |
| Digital loans carry a cooling-off/look-up window: minimum 1 day for loans under 7-day tenor, minimum 3 days for loans of 7 days or more; borrower may exit by repaying principal + proportionate APR, no penalty (Slide 8) | RBI FAQs on Digital Lending Guidelines; carried into RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 | FAQ: February 2023; Directions effective 8 May 2025 |
| RBI operates a public directory of Digital Lending Apps (DLAs) so borrowers can check if an app is tied to a regulated lender (Slide 7) | RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 | Directory live from 1 July 2025 |
| "Small first loan, fast repayment, automatically raised limit" is a common pattern across short-tenor loan apps (Slide 5, 6) | General, widely reported industry pattern; not tied to a single named company or statistic | — |
