## Claims researched but excluded from this carousel

- Aggregator-blog market-size figures for India's personal loan market (a claimed "₹1.36 lakh crore in 2024, projected to reach ₹4.3 lakh crore by 2025, 32% growth") were internally inconsistent and not sourced from RBI, NSE/BSE, or a company filing. Not used anywhere in this carousel.
- Aggregator-blog figures on the share of fintech loans under ₹5,000 or the fintech/NBFC share of small-ticket lending were sourced secondhand from blog posts referencing an Experian whitepaper, not the primary report itself. Not used anywhere in this carousel.
- The RBI First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG) 5% cap (RBI Guidelines on Default Loss Guarantee in Digital Lending, June 8, 2023) was researched as useful context on why digital lenders are structurally incentivised toward speed and volume, but was ultimately left out of the final 9-slide cut to keep the carousel to one idea (the KFS and cooling-off window). It remains available in research_notes/2026-09-26_loan-app-cooling-off-window_research.md if a future carousel wants to build on it.
