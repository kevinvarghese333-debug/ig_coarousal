That loan-app notification is designed to make "get money now" feel like the only decision in the room. It is not the only decision. RBI requires every digital lender to show you a Key Fact Statement before you sign, stating the real annual cost, not the EMI you see on the screen. It also gives you a cooling-off window after you sign: 3 days if the loan runs a week or longer, 1 day if it is shorter. Exit in that window and you pay only what you borrowed plus the proportionate interest. No penalty.

Most people never open the Key Fact Statement. Most people do not know the window exists at all. By the time the EMI starts to pinch, it has already closed.

This is not a warning to avoid loan apps. It is a reminder that speed and fairness are not the same thing, and that RBI already built you an exit ramp. Use it while it is open.
