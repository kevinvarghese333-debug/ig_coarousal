"No Cost EMI" is one of the most tapped buttons in Indian retail, and one of the least understood. The zero percent framing is not a lie exactly. It is a rename. Banks do not lend for free, so when the interest line reads zero, that cost has usually moved into the price you would have paid in full, in the form of a discount you quietly gave up.

The RBI flagged this back in 2013, when it found several "0% interest" schemes were really interest dressed up as processing fees. The label has since gotten smoother. The underlying math has not changed.

The bigger cost is not even the money. It is the missing pause. Full payment forces you to ask if you can afford something today. EMI removes that question exactly when the amount is largest.

Before your next "No Cost EMI" tap, ask for the full upfront price first. Compare the two numbers. The gap is where the discount went.

Do you check the full price before choosing EMI, or does the zero percent line settle it for you?
