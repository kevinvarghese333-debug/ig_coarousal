# Sources and Fact Check: The Interest Never Disappeared. It Moved.

## Claims used on-slide and their support

1. **Mechanism claim (Slides 3-5): the bank still charges interest on a "no cost EMI" loan; the merchant pays that interest to the bank; the merchant typically recovers it by withdrawing the cash discount it would otherwise offer for full payment.**
   Support: consistent across every source consulted, including product documentation from Paytm Business describing EMI subvention, and multiple independent finance explainers. No single fragile figure is required for this claim; it describes a mechanism, not a statistic.

2. **Regulatory claim (Slide 9, caption): the RBI made the point, around 2013, that "zero percent interest" is not really zero, after finding banks camouflaging interest as processing fees.**
   Support: reported consistently by Business Standard and Moneylife, both referencing an RBI circular from around September 2013.
   Status: **[VERIFY]** — only secondary reporting was found in this research pass, not the RBI's own circular text. Confirm the exact circular number and date on rbi.org.in before this carousel is published.

3. **Illustrative rupee example (Slide 5): a ₹60,000 phone, paid in full for ₹57,000 after a ₹3,000 cash discount, versus ₹60,000 total under no-cost EMI.**
   Status: explicitly illustrative, not a real bank, retailer or product's terms. Labelled as such on the slide itself. Does not require a source.

## Claims deliberately excluded from this carousel

- Any specific GST rate or amount applied to the interest/processing component of an EMI conversion. Mentioned in secondary finance blogs but not traced to a primary GST/CBIC notification in this research pass, and tax treatment can change. Excluded per the rule against presenting unverified or fragile tax figures as fact.
- Any specific per-instalment processing fee amount or range (secondary sources cited figures as wide as ₹99 to ₹999 per month). Fees vary by bank, card and merchant tie-up and were judged too fragile and variable to state as fact. Excluded from all on-slide copy and the caption.
- No specific bank, NBFC or retailer is named anywhere in the carousel or caption.
