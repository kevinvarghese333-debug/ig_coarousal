You tap "0% EMI" because it feels like the safer button. No interest, no cost, no downside. Except a bank never lends for free. When there is truly no interest on paper, the cost has usually moved somewhere else: a cash discount you did not get, a processing fee, GST on that fee, or simply the extra confidence EMI gives you to buy something you would have paused on in cash.

None of this means EMI is bad. It means "no cost" is a marketing phrase, not a financial fact, and RBI has been saying so since 2013. The fix is not to avoid EMI. It is to ask for the full cash price before you decide, so you are comparing two real numbers instead of one real number and one flattering one.

This is not a call to avoid EMI or a specific lender. It is a habit: ask the question before you tap the button.
