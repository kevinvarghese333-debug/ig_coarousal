# Sources and Fact Check: No Cost EMI Is Not a Free Purchase

Full research trail: `research_notes/2026-08-28_no-cost-emi-hidden-cost_research.md`

## Claims used on-slide (verified)

1. **Slide 1 and Slide 4** — "In 2013, RBI stated that zero percent interest does not exist, and that the cost of these schemes was often recovered from the customer rather than genuinely waived."
   - Business Standard, 25 Sep 2013: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/reuters/rbi-asks-banks-to-charge-uniform-interest-rate-on-retail-emi-schemes-113092500642_1.html
   - Business Standard, 25 Sep 2013: https://www.business-standard.com/article/finance/rbi-cracks-whip-on-retail-finance-schemes-with-hidden-cost-113092500706_1.html
   - Moneylife, 2013: https://www.moneylife.in/article/rbi-says-no-zero-percent-interest-scheme-for-buying-consumer-goods/34611.html

2. **Slide 7** — "The label moved from 'Zero Percent EMI' to 'No Cost EMI' after RBI's intervention, with the same underlying mechanism."
   - Corroborated by the same 2013 reporting above plus general industry explainers (bankbazaar.com, cardcheck.in) describing "No Cost EMI" as the current branding for the same class of scheme.

## Numbers NOT claimed as real (used only as illustration)

- Slide 5 and Slide 6's ₹40,000 phone / ₹38,000 cash price / ₹2,000 discount are explicitly labelled "Illustrative numbers, not a live offer." No specific bank, retailer, or product is named or implied.
