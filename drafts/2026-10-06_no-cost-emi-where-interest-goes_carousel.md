# No-Cost EMI: Who Pays the Interest?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI during festive sale season
- Central idea: No-cost EMI does not remove the interest. It moves it, often into the cash discount you give up.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians aged 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: "Zero interest" sounds free, but a lender is still being paid
- Primary engagement goal: Saves and comments
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-step check before the next sale, a myth friends believe, and a real judgement question

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: No-cost EMI. So who pays the interest?
2. Contrarian hook: No-cost EMI is not a free decision.
3. Relatable-pain hook: Your phone EMI looks small. Check the cash price.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: Where the interest hides in a no-cost EMI.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: Read this before the festive sale EMI banner.

**Selected hook:** No-cost EMI. So who pays the interest?

**Why it wins:** It names a phrase the viewer sees every sale and turns it into a question they cannot answer yet.
