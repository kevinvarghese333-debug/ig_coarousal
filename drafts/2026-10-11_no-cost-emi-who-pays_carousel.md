# No-Cost EMI: Who Pays the Cost?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI at festive-sale checkouts
- Central idea: "No-cost" EMI is a price question, not an interest question, so compare the cash price with the EMI total.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians aged 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: The offer feels free, yet the cost can sit in a lost cash discount or fee
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, with comments as secondary
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question rule to reuse at checkout; a relatable "bought it because the EMI looked small" prompt.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: "No-cost EMI. So who pays the cost?"
2. Contrarian hook: "Zero-cost EMI is not always a free decision."
3. Relatable-pain hook: "The EMI looked small. The phone was not."
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: "How a seller pays your interest and still wins."
5. Warning-or-risk hook: "Check this before you tap no-cost EMI."

**Selected hook:** "No-cost" EMI. So who pays the cost?

**Why it wins:** It turns a phrase the viewer has seen at checkout into an open question they cannot answer yet.
