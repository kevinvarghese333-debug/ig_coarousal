# Zero-cost EMI is not always free

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: Zero-cost EMI
- Central idea: "Zero-cost" EMI moves the cost somewhere else instead of removing it.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried viewers aged 21 to 40 shopping in sale season
- Audience tension: The monthly number feels small, so the price stops being questioned.
- Primary engagement goal: Comments and saves
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-point checklist, a relatable sale-banner moment and a real either/or question.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: Zero-cost EMI. So who is paying?
2. Contrarian hook: Zero-cost EMI is not always free.
3. Relatable-pain hook: ₹10,000 a month felt easy. It was not.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: How zero-cost EMI still earns someone money.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: Check this before you tap "EMI".

**Selected hook:** Zero-cost EMI is not always free.

**Why it wins:** It is specific, instantly recognisable and corrects a belief the viewer holds.
