# Who Pays the Interest on a "No-Cost" EMI?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: A "no-cost" EMI still has interest; a discount hides it, and GST or fees can sit outside the discount.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians aged 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on card EMI
- Audience tension: It feels free, yet the bill sometimes says otherwise.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, then comments
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question checklist for sale season; a concrete bill-surprise question.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity: Who pays the interest on a "no-cost" EMI?
2. Contrarian: No-cost EMI is not a no-cost decision.
3. Relatable pain: The EMI screen said ₹0 interest. The bill said otherwise.
4. Hidden mechanism: The interest is not gone. It is hiding in a discount.
5. Warning: Read the whole receipt before you tap "No-cost".

**Selected hook:** Who pays the interest on a "no-cost" EMI?

**Why it wins:** It is one clear question about a thing the viewer has actually tapped.
