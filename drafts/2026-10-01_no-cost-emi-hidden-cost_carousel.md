# No-cost EMI. Someone still gets paid.

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: "No-cost EMI" rarely means no cost; the cost moves into the price you could have paid today.
- Primary audience: Indian salaried viewers, 21 to 40, buying phones and appliances on card EMI
- Audience tension: The checkout screen says interest is zero, so it feels like a free decision.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves and shares
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question checklist to save, a warning to send to someone buying on EMI, and a comment question about misleading offers.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: Zero interest. So where is the cost?
2. Contrarian hook: "No-cost EMI" is not free.
3. Relatable-pain hook: Your ₹10,000 EMI looks light. Check the price.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: No-cost EMI. Someone still gets paid.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: Before you tap "no-cost EMI", read this.

**Selected hook:** No-cost EMI. Someone still gets paid.

**Why it wins:** It names a familiar label and implies a hidden party, which creates tension without fear.
