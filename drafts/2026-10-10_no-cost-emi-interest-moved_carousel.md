# No-cost EMI: the interest did not vanish, it moved

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: "No-cost" EMI is not interest-free; the interest is moved into a discount you give up, plus GST and fees that can still show up.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians aged 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on card EMI
- Audience tension: It says zero, so it feels free
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, with comments on cash price versus EMI
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question checkout checklist, a myth friends believe, and a real judgement question

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity: Where does the interest in a no-cost EMI go?
2. Contrarian: "No-cost" EMI. The interest did not vanish. It moved.
3. Relatable pain: You tapped no-cost EMI. Did you check the cash price?
4. Hidden mechanism: The discount you lose is the interest you "don't" pay.
5. Warning: No-cost EMI can still add GST to your bill.

**Selected hook:** "No-cost" EMI. The interest did not vanish. It moved.

**Why it wins:** It corrects a belief the viewer holds and promises a specific answer on the next swipe.
