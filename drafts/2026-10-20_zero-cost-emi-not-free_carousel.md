# "No-cost" EMI still has a cost

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: No-cost EMI
- Central idea: A "no-cost" EMI usually moves the interest into a seller discount, and fees, GST or a lost cash discount can still leave a cost.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on EMI
- Audience tension: It looks free, so the decision feels risk-free.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves and comments
- Why people may save, share or comment: A three-question checkout rule, a relatable sale-season scene, and a real judgement question.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity: Where does the interest in "no-cost" EMI go?
2. Contrarian: "No-cost" EMI still has a cost.
3. Relatable-pain: Six EMIs of ₹10,000 felt painless. Check the receipt.
4. Hidden-business-mechanism: The seller discount is your interest, handed back.
5. Warning-or-risk: Before you tap EMI, check three numbers.

**Selected hook:** "No-cost" EMI still has a cost.

**Why it wins:** It corrects a belief the viewer holds at every sale, in six words.

## Caption

You see "No-cost EMI" at checkout and the monthly number looks painless. So the decision feels free.

Usually, the lender is still charging interest. The seller then trims the price by a similar amount, so the EMIs add up to the headline price. The cost has been moved, not removed.

What can still sit on top: GST on the interest, a processing fee, and the cash discount you could have got by paying upfront. Terms differ by lender, product and seller, so check the current terms on your own offer.

The number in the carousel is an illustration with an assumed 15% rate and 6 months. It is not a real offer.

The habit matters more than the fee. A big price turns into a small monthly number, and "can I afford this?" quietly becomes "can I afford the EMI?"

Before you tap EMI, ask for the cash price, the total on EMI, and every fee and tax added.

If paying cash was ₹500 cheaper, would you still take the no-cost EMI? Tell me why.
