# No-Cost EMI: Who Is Paying the Cost?

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: Zero or no-cost EMI offers
- Central idea: A "no-cost" EMI usually still carries a cost, moved into a lost discount, a fee or a higher price.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indians aged 21 to 40 buying phones and appliances on instalments
- Audience tension: The offer feels free, so nobody compares prices.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves, with shares to friends who always choose EMI
- Why people may save, share or comment: It gives a three-question checklist for the next checkout and invites people to share their own EMI choices.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: No-cost EMI. Who is paying the cost?
2. Contrarian hook: Zero-cost EMI is not always a free decision.
3. Relatable-pain hook: You tapped EMI because it said "no cost".
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: Where the interest goes when an EMI says zero.
5. Warning-or-risk hook: The price tag behind "no-cost" EMI.

**Selected hook:** No-cost EMI. Who is paying the cost?

**Why it wins:** It is a familiar checkout moment, takes under a second to read and leaves one open question.
