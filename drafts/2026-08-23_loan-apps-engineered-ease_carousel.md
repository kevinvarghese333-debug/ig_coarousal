# Borrowing Got Easier. The Cost Didn't Get Smaller.

## Status

Production complete. Review before Instagram publishing.

## Strategic Premise

- Topic: Why instant loan apps are designed to remove friction from borrowing, and what that friction was quietly protecting you from.
- Central idea: The ease of taking a small loan app loan is a deliberate business design choice, not a convenience, and the two real protections that exist against it (the RBI-mandated APR disclosure and the cooling-off window) are the parts almost nobody checks.
- Primary audience: Salaried Indian professionals, 21 to 40, who have taken or been offered an instant loan-app credit line and never read the fine print.
- Audience tension: They know loan apps feel "too easy." They have never had the mechanism explained, or been shown the actual annualised cost versus the daily-fee framing.
- Primary engagement goal: Saves (this is a checklist people will want to reference before their next loan-app notification) and shares (a warning worth sending to someone who overuses these apps).
- Why people may save, share or comment: It reframes a familiar, slightly guilty habit (tapping "accept" on a loan-app offer) as a designed business outcome, hands over a concrete, non-judgmental checklist, and closes with a comment question that invites people to admit their own behaviour.

## Five Cover Hooks

1. Curiosity hook: "The one screen a loan app never makes you look at."
2. Contrarian hook: "Borrowing got easier. The cost didn't get smaller."
3. Relatable-pain hook: "You checked if it would approve you. You didn't check what it would cost."
4. Hidden-business-mechanism hook: "Removing friction from a loan isn't kindness. It's the business model."
5. Warning-or-risk hook: "You get days, sometimes just one, to undo a loan-app decision. Most people never use them."

**Selected hook:** Borrowing got easier. The cost didn't get smaller.

**Why it wins:** It states a contrarian tension in seven words that anyone who has used a loan app immediately recognises, without giving away the mechanism, which is what earns the swipe.

## Caption

Loan apps didn't get generous. They got efficient.

Every step that used to slow a loan down, paperwork, a bank visit, a waiting period, gave you a moment to reconsider. Remove the step and you remove the moment.

That's not an accusation. It's a design choice, and a reasonable one from a business's side: less friction means more loans taken, faster. But it means the responsibility for checking the real cost has moved entirely to you.

Two things already exist to help. Since October 2024, RBI requires lenders to show you a Key Facts Statement with the loan's true annualised cost (APR), not just a daily or flat fee. And every digital loan comes with a cooling-off window, as short as a single day for short-tenure loans, where you can exit by repaying only the principal and proportionate APR.

Neither helps if you don't know to look for it.

Have you ever checked the APR on a loan-app offer before accepting it, or just checked whether it approved you?
